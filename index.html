Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is pest control?

A: Pest control involves identifying, managing, and preventing unwanted pests such as cockroaches, ants, termites, rodents, and mosquitoes in residential and commercial properties.

Q: Why is professional pest control important in Singapore?

A: Professional pest management can help identify pest activity, reduce infestations, and recommend suitable prevention and treatment methods for different environments.

Q: What types of pests can be controlled?

A: Common pest management services may cover cockroaches, termites, ants, rodents, bed bugs, mosquitoes, and other unwanted pests.

Q: How can I prevent pests at home?

A: Keep food properly stored, maintain cleanliness, remove standing water, seal possible entry points, and inspect areas where pests may hide.

Q: When should I contact a pest control professional?

A: Contact a qualified pest management professional if you notice recurring pest activity, property damage, nests, droppings, or signs of a growing infestation.
